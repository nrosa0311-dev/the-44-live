Age Policy · The 44 · Glendale, AZ

Is The 44 18 And Over?

No. The 44 is a 21 and over venue. You must be 21 or older, with a valid government-issued photo ID, to get in any day we are open. The only exception is a ticketed concert that sets a lower age limit, which is listed on that show's ticket page.

The 44 is a live music bar at 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale, Arizona, five minutes from Glendale Community College and a short drive from Grand Canyon University. It is 21 and over at all times, every day it is open.

Ticketed Concerts Set Their Own Age

The 44 is a 21 and over venue, but a ticketed concert can set a different age limit, so a specific show may admit guests under 21. That is the only way to get in under 21.

The age for any specific show is printed on that show's ticket page. If you are under 21 and coming out for a named act, check the ticket page first.

Bring A Real ID, Every Day

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID is required at the door every day. Arizona law requires The 44 to verify age before serving alcohol, so guests under 21 are not served.
